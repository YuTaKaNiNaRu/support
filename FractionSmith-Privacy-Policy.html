Privacy Policy for FractionSmith

Last Updated: October 2024
ZERO DATA COLLECTION

1. Overview & Core Commitment

FractionSmith ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a professional utility for carpenters, woodworkers, and builders. We believe that your project measurements, project plans, and calculations are strictly personal and private.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information or usage data. The App functions 100% locally on your iOS device and does not require an internet connection to perform its core calculations.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • No Accounts Required: You can use FractionSmith without creating an account, logging in, or providing an email address.
  • Local Calculations: All calculations—including fractions, stair layouts, baluster spacing, diagonals, and 1D cut list bin-packing—run strictly on-device using local algorithms. None of your inputs leave your device.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or behavioral telemetry tools.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

FractionSmith offers an optional Lifetime Pro upgrade ("Unlock Forever"). All transactions are processed directly by Apple Inc. via the App Store (StoreKit).

  • We do not process, collect, or store your credit card, billing address, or payment details.
  • Payment management and receipt validation are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Backups

Any project parameters, presets, or history stored within the app are saved strictly in your device's local sandbox storage. If you enable iOS iCloud Backups, this local data may be backed up to your personal Apple iCloud storage in accordance with Apple's standard device backup protocol.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is an engineering calculation tool and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13.
