FeedSmith Privacy Policy

100% Offline • Zero Data Collection

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

FeedSmith ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines our complete non-collection of user data.

2. Zero Data Collection & Local-First Architecture

FeedSmith operates entirely on a local-first architecture. We do not collect, track, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, farm metrics, animal data, ration formulas, or location details. All computations and saved formulation data remain strictly within your device's secure local sandbox.

3. Network & Offline Operation

The app requires no internet connection to balance rations or compute nutritional values. No user inputs are ever sent to external cloud servers.

4. In-App Purchases

All financial transactions (such as Pro Edition purchases) are processed directly by Apple through the official App Store StoreKit infrastructure. We never have access to your credit card or billing details.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

FeedSmith does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or behavioral trackers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the official support channels listed on the App Store product page.
